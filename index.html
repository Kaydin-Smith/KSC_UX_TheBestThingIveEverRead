The Silence Of Our Friends

“The Silence of Our Friends” is a graphic novel exploring racism and racial violence, particularly in the South during the civil rights movement. The story revolves around two families, one white and one Black, with their patriarchs being the main protagonists. Jack Long, a white reporter living in a majority-white neighbourhood in Texas, has taken a new reporting job. His family consists of a son, a daughter, and his wife. Larry Thomas, a Black professor and activist living across racial lines in Houston, has a similarly nuclear family. Two very similar families have vastly different experiences because of race.

The tragedy that triggers the story is a protest at a campus that ends in five false murder allegations against Black students, which is the story Jack is reporting on. The story follows the families' growing trust and friendship with each other, rising racial tensions, and false allegations. The final chapter, however, takes place the day of Martin Luther King Jr’s assassination and ends with a quote of his: “In the end, we will remember not the words of our enemies, but the silence of our friends.”
